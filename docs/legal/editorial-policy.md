# Editorial Policy — The Eagle's Eye Media

**Effective Date:** January 2025  
**Platform:** `theeagleseyemedia.com`  
**Organization:** The Eagle's Eye Media — Cameroon

---

## 1. Purpose

This Editorial Policy governs the journalistic standards, ethical obligations, and content guidelines for all editorial content published by **The Eagle's Eye Media**. It applies to editors, authors, contributors, editors-in-chief, and any automated or AI-assisted tools used in content creation.

---

## 2. Editorial Mission

The Eagle's Eye Media is a parliamentary news and civic information platform. Our mission is to:

- Inform the public on parliamentary, legislative, senatorial, and governance affairs in Cameroon.
- Promote accountability and transparency in public office.
- Provide context-driven, fact-based analysis of national policy.
- Serve as a bridge between parliamentary institutions and the Cameroonian public.

---

## 3. Core Principles

### 3.1 Accuracy & Verification
- All claims are verified against primary sources: parliamentary records, official gazettes, verified press releases, or direct official statements.
- "Unconfirmed" or unverified claims must be clearly labeled as such.
- Real-time breaking alerts (Bills, Votes, Statements) are flagged clearly as **BREAKING** until confirmed.

### 3.2 Independence
- Editorial decisions are made independently of advertisers, sponsors, political actors, or government influence.
- No external party may dictate story selection, framing, or publication timing.

### 3.3 Impartiality & Fairness
- Present significant competing perspectives where they exist.
- Afford subjects of critical reporting an opportunity to respond before publication, where safety and timing permit.
- Balance is required on controversial issues; opinion pieces are clearly labeled.

### 3.4 Transparency
- Authors and Editorial Board are identifiable.
- Corrections policy: errata are appended to articles via a visible **Correction Notice**.
- The role of AI tools in any article is disclosed when AI materially shapes content.

## 5. Fact-Checking Protocol

1. **Source Triage** — Primary source first (parliamentary record, official social, press release from official account). Secondary (wire service, reputable outlet). Tertiary (social media, unverified).
2. **Corroboration** — At least two independent sources for serious allegations.
3. **Documentation** — Every factual claim must trace to a verifiable source.
4. **Cease & Desist Contact** — If contacted by a subject for correction, the Editorial Board evaluates and responds within 10 business days.

---

## 6. Corrections & Updates

- **Minor corrections** (typos, formatting, dates) may be made silently with an "Updated" timestamp.
- **Substantive corrections** (wrong names, misquotes, incorrect statistics) require a **Correction Notice** appended to the article, visible for at least 30 days.
- **Retractions** — In cases of materially false reporting, a standalone **Retraction Statement** is published and linked prominently.

---

## 7. Confidentiality & Sourcing

- Sources must never be put at undue risk.
- Names and identifiers are withheld when safety is a concern.
- "Editor's Note" used when a source's identity is materially relevant but cannot be disclosed.
- Recordings and source data must be kept securely and not shared externally without consent.

---

## 8. Conflict of Interest

| Relationship | Disclosure Required |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Author holds public office | Mandatory |
| Author's family is a parliamentary staffer | Mandatory |
| Author was a former employee of a covered ministry | Mandatory |
| Author received payment from a covered entity | Mandatory |
| Author has no connection | No disclosure needed |

- Contributors must disclose potential conflicts before assignment or publication.
- Failure to disclose may result in removal from the byline and review by the Editorial Board.

---

## 9. AI Usage in Journalism

- AI tools may be used for **translation, summarization, headline generation, transcribing, and research support**.
- AI is **never** the final decision-maker on whether content is published.
- AI-assisted content is reviewed by a human editor for accuracy, bias, and legal risk.
- If AI contributed material framing or analysis, that is disclosed to readers (e.g., "AI-assisted draft, reviewed by [Editor Name]").

---

## 10. Complaints & Ombudsman

- All complaints may be submitted to `complaints@theeagleseyemedia.com`.
- Complaints are reviewed within **10 business days**.
- A formal editorial response is issued within **20 business days**.
- Escalation to the Editor-in-Chief is free of charge.
- Serious complaints (plagiarism, defamation, privacy breach) are escalated immediately.

---

## 11. Hiring & Editorial Standards

- Writers must demonstrate knowledge of parliamentary procedure, Cameroonian governance, and journalistic ethics.
- Training on source protection, fact-checking, and media law is provided to all contributors.
- The Eagle's Eye Media is an equal-opportunity publisher and welcomes diverse voices.

---

## 12. Governing Body

Policy is owned and enforced by the **Editor-in-Chief** and **Editorial Board**. Amendments require a majority Board vote and are published on this page within 5 business days of adoption.
