# Privacy Policy — The Eagle's Eye Media

**Effective Date:** January 2025  
**Platform:** `theeagleseyemedia.com`  
**Operator:** The Eagle's Eye Media, Cameroon

---

## 1. What This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy explains how **The Eagle's Eye Media** ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use our Platform.

We are committed to safeguarding your privacy in compliance with applicable Cameroonian data protection principles and best practices.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly
| Data Point | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| Name / Display Name | Account identification |
| Email Address | Authentication, newsletter delivery, support |
| Password (hashed) | Account security |
| Phone Number (optional) | MFA / password recovery |

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
| Data Point | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| IP Address | Security, rate-limiting, geo-insights |
| Browser / Device Info | Compatibility and analytics |
| Page Views & Clicks | Content performance analytics |
| Session Tokens (JWT) | Authentication (30-min expiry) |

### 2.3 AI Assist Interactions
Queries submitted to the AI search and explain features may be logged to improve performance. **No personal identifiers** are attached to AI query logs.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- **Service delivery** — to authenticate users, process newsletter subscriptions, and deliver articles
- **Security** — to detect unauthorized access, enforce rate limits, and prevent abuse
- **Improvements** — to understand content engagement and improve user experience
- **Communication** — to send newsletters, password reset links, or important service updates

We **do not** sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

---

## 4. Data Storage & Security

- User data is stored via **Supabase** (PostgreSQL) with industry-standard encryption.
- Passwords are **never stored in plaintext** — hashed via Supabase Auth (bcrypt).
- JWT session tokens expire after **30 minutes** and are stored client-side in `localStorage`.
- All API communications use HTTPS in production.

---

## 5. Data Sharing

Personal information may be shared only in the following circumstances:
- **With your consent**
- **To comply with legal obligations** (Cameroonian law, court orders)
- **To protect our rights or the safety of users** (anti-abuse, fraud prevention)
- **With service providers** (hosting, email delivery) who are bound by confidentiality agreements

---

## 6. Cookies & Local Storage

The Platform uses:
- **Essential cookies/localStorage** for session management and authentication
- **Analytical data** (via query metrics) to understand page performance
- **No advertising or tracking cookies** from third-party ad networks

You may disable localStorage in your browser, but some Platform features (login, AI Assist, preferences) may not function correctly.

---

## 7. Your Rights

Under applicable law, you have the right to:
- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Delete** your account and associated data
- **Export** your data in a portable format
- **Withdraw consent** for newsletter or AI features at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us via the Platform's support channels.

---

## 8. Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Active user accounts | Until account deletion |
| Newsletter subscribers | Until unsubscribe |
| Article comments | Until content removal |
| AI query logs | 90 days (anonymized) |
| Audit logs | 12 months (admin actions) |

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If such data is identified, it will be deleted promptly.
